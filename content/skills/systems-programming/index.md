# Overview

Systems programming is the discipline that most changed how I think about software.

Instead of viewing programs as isolated applications, I began studying how operating systems, memory, networking, storage and runtime environments work together to support modern software.

Most of my current technical growth comes from understanding these lower-level concepts through practical engineering projects.

---

# Why Systems Programming?

My interest developed while moving away from purely academic programming toward building production-oriented software.

Learning Rust exposed concepts such as ownership, borrowing, concurrency and memory management that encouraged a deeper understanding of software than I had previously encountered.

That naturally expanded into studying operating systems, networking, distributed systems and backend infrastructure.
