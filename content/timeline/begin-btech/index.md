Beginning my B.Tech in Computer Engineering marked the transition from learning programming as coursework to viewing software engineering as a long-term career.

The early semesters focused on building a foundation through C, Python, Java, databases and operating systems while gradually becoming more comfortable with Linux and Git.

Outside formal coursework I started experimenting with personal projects, explored self-hosting concepts and became increasingly interested in backend systems rather than frontend development.

Although my understanding of software engineering was still limited, this period established the foundation upon which every later project would build.
