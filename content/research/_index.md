# Coursework & Academic Research

This section gathers research prototypes and technical studies completed in collaboration with peers and faculty at the Department of Computer Engineering, Vidyalankar Institute of Technology (VIT), Mumbai.

An applied cybersecurity research prototype and machine learning pipeline exploring pre-scoring CVSS v3.1 estimation and publication-time exploit prediction (EPSS/KEV).
* **Code:** [github.com/seucra/vulnarability-prioritization-triage-system ↗](https://github.com/seucra/vulnarability-prioritization-triage-system)
* **Research Paper:** [VTS Research Paper (PDF) ↗](https://github.com/seucra/vulnarability-prioritization-triage-system/blob/main/docs/research/vts_research_paper.pdf)

* **Authors:** Ashish Chaudhari, Dibyaranjan Prusty, Khan Rayed, Shams Tabrez Ahmed (VIT Mumbai)
