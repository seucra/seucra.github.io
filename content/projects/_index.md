# Projects & Experiments

This page gathers the software projects I've worked on across backend systems, Rust, WebAssembly, and cybersecurity.

Rather than just building features for resume bullet points, I use these projects to explore real engineering trade-offs: dealing with distributed protocols, structuring clean interfaces, and understanding how memory, networks, and operating systems behave underneath.
